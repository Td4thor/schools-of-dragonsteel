# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-17) — first release

Ice and Fire: Schools of Dragonsteel gives Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks' Holy, Ender, Blood, Evocation, Nature and
Eldritch schools their own dragonsteel.

- **School dragon blood.** Pour Dragon's Breath into a cauldron, cast any spell of a school at it, and fill bottles
  with that school's blood.
- **Tempering.** Iron ingots and school blood in an Ice and Fire dragon forge, breathed on by a dragon, make tempered
  school steel at Ice and Fire's dragonsteel pace (50 s an ingot).
- **Finishing.** A spell of the school cast at Legendary rarity, aimed at the forge, finishes up to a stack of
  tempered steel into dragonsteel. Apex Arcana's Apex spells finish it too, and the optional **Apex Only** add-on jar
  makes them the only way (server-side; clients are told the rule).
- **Gear for every school.** Dragonsteel armour with Dragon Priest stats (+150 max mana, +5% spell power and +10%
  school power a piece), each set with its own look; dragonsteel tools; a Dragon Priest staff upgraded in the forge from
  Ice and Fire: Spellbooks' base staff; and six swords:
  - **Dawn** (Holy): holy fire that water cannot put out, extra damage to undead.
  - **Elsewhere** (Ender): while held, arrows and spell projectiles miss and you step aside like an enderman. In the
    hand its blade hovers in shards that fly apart on every swing and shatter in every direction when it dodges.
  - **Thirst** (Blood): heals you for a fifth of the damage its hits deal.
  - **Covenant** (Evocation): a fully charged hit calls a row of evoker fangs.
  - **Briar** (Nature): hits poison and slow.
  - **Hush** (Eldritch): hits deal extra damage armour cannot stop and wrap players in Darkness.
- **JEI pages** (optional): School Dragon Blood and Dragonsteel Finishing categories, with a page for every spell that
  finishes each school's steel, and an information page on every item. Ice and Fire's own JEI plugin shows the forge
  recipes.
- Configurable: the finishing rule and spell lists, ranges, armour stats and every sword's numbers (`config/dragonsteel_schools-common.toml`).
