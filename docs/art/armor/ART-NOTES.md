# Plate armour art notes

Generated by `python tools/generate_armor.py`; previews by `tools/render_armor.py` (software renders, not in-game).

## Rig
Iron's Spells `GenericCustomArmorRenderer` (GeckoLib 4.8.4) with an `AutoGlowingGeoLayer`. Bones: `armorHead`,
`armorBody`, `armorRightArm`/`armorLeftArm`, `armorRightLeg`/`armorLeftLeg`, `armorRightBoot`/`armorLeftBoot`,
plus Iron's extras `armorLeggingTorsoLayer` (belt and faulds, shown with leggings) and
`armorTorsoExtensionRightLeg`/`LeftLeg` (tassets hanging from the cuirass, shown with the chestplate, following the legs).

| Set | Cubes | Texture | Glow texels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holy | 77 (helm 38, incl. halo ring and rays) | 128x128 | 462 |
| Ender | 46 | 128x128 | 148 |

Box UV, one texel per model unit. Glowmask texels copy the base colour (GeckoLib draws the base texture full-bright
wherever the mask is opaque).

## Design
- **Holy, radiant heavy full plate.** White-silver plate with gold trim: great helm with a visor slit, gold keel and
  crest comb, side breathing slits and a glowing sunburst halo behind the head; gorget; breastplate with a glowing
  sunburst and sun medallion; triple-layer pauldrons; couters, vambraces, faulds, tassets, poleyns, greaves and
  sabatons. Sun, ray and wing motifs only (no crosses).
- **Ender, lean End-assassin plate.** Obsidian-black and void-purple segmented plate: plated hood over a closed mask
  with a glowing violet visor slit, lamellar cuirass with a diagonal purpur sash, asymmetric shoulders (small right
  cap, layered left pauldron with an End-stone shard), slim greaves, glowing End glyphs.

## Verified
1. Both `.geo.json` files parse and bake with the real GeckoLib 4.8.4 classes (`GeometryTree` +
   `BakedModelFactory`), and every required bone exists with the right parent (scratch `BakeProbe`).
2. The generator refuses UV overlaps, unpainted texels and fractional box sizes, and nudges cubes so no two armour
   faces are coplanar where they overlap (no z-fighting between armour pieces).
3. Software renders: `holy_full_set.png`, `holy_slots.png`, `ender_full_set.png`, `ender_slots.png`.
