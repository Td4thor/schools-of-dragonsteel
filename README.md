# Ice and Fire: Schools of Dragonsteel

A Forge 1.20.1 addon for Ice and Fire and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, by Td4thor and Hastur. Ice and Fire has fire,
ice and lightning dragonsteel; this mod gives Iron's other schools their own: **Holy, Ender, Blood, Evocation, Nature
and Eldritch**, each with dragonsteel armour, a sword with its own power, four tools and a Dragon Priest staff.

## How it works

1. **School blood.** Pour Dragon's Breath into a cauldron (one bottle per level) and cast any spell of a school at it.
   The breath becomes that school's dragon blood; fill glass bottles from it.
2. **Tempering.** Iron ingots and school blood in an Ice and Fire dragon forge. While a dragon breathes on it, the forge
   tempers one ingot per 50 s, as with Ice and Fire's own dragonsteel.
3. **Finishing.** Look at the forge and cast a spell of that school at Legendary rarity (or an Apex Arcana Apex spell).
   Up to a stack of tempered steel becomes dragonsteel. The optional Apex Only add-on jar makes Apex spells the only way.
4. **Gear.** Armour, sword and tools from the ingots; the staff from Ice and Fire: Spellbooks' Dragon Priest Staff and
   school blood in the forge.

With JEI installed, the School Dragon Blood and Dragonsteel Finishing pages list every step and every spell that works,
and each item has an information page.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4+
- Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks 3.16.3 (required), GeckoLib 4.4+
- Ice and Fire 2.1.13 (needed for the dragon forge)
- Optional: Ice and Fire: Spellbooks 2.3.2+ (the staves), Apex Arcana 1.3.0+ (Apex spells), JEI

## Building

Copy the third-party jars into `libs/` (`python tools/prepare_dependencies.py`), then run
`./gradlew build verifyGameTests` with JDK 17. Art and data are generated by `tools/generate_textures.py` and
`tools/generate_data.py`; see `docs/TESTING.md` for the test plan and `docs/PORTING-IAF2.md` for the Ice and Fire 2 port.
