# Plate armour art notes

Generated by `python tools/generate_armor.py`. Previews come from `tools/render_armor.py` (software renders) and,
for real in-game lighting and glow, from `bash tools/run_client_qa.sh` (armour stands on a sky platform, see
`docs/TESTING.md`). Version 2 dates from 0.1.0-dev.3.

## Rig
Iron's Spells `GenericCustomArmorRenderer` (GeckoLib 4.8.4) with an `AutoGlowingGeoLayer`. Bones: `armorHead`,
`armorBody`, `armorRightArm`/`armorLeftArm`, `armorRightLeg`/`armorLeftLeg`, `armorRightBoot`/`armorLeftBoot`,
plus Iron's extras `armorLeggingTorsoLayer` (belt and faulds, shown with leggings) and
`armorTorsoExtensionRightLeg`/`LeftLeg` (tassets and coat tails hanging from the cuirass, shown with the chestplate,
following the legs).

Box UV, one texel per model unit. Glowmask texels copy the base colour (GeckoLib draws the base texture full-bright
wherever the mask is opaque).

## Painting rules (v2)
- Plates are lit from above: a bright top rim, darker foot rows, a shaded far edge and one vertical sheen column on
  wide faces. No random speckle; at armour scale it read as dirt.
- Overlapping lames are separated by a shadow line under each lip, not by a trim stripe.
- Accent metal (Holy gold, Ender amethyst) goes only on edges that mean something. In v1, trim on every cube's top
  and bottom made both sets read as striped.
- Glow is a few deliberate accents, never a fill.
- No crosses, including accidental ones. A 3x3 diamond is a plus sign, and a centre seam under an emblem or a keel
  through a brow band reads as a cross; check the front and back in game.

## Design
- **Holy, radiant heavy full plate.** White steel with burnished gold edges. A great helm with a gold brow, a glowing
  visor slit, flank breathing slits, a crest comb, a sun on the back and a sunburst halo. A gorget. A breastplate
  with an eight-ray glowing sun round a sun medallion, and a winged backplate. Domed pauldrons with an upstanding
  gardbrace and two flaring lames, the lower one with a radiant hem. Then couters, vambraces, faulds, tassets, gold
  poleyns, greaves and sabatons.
- **Ender, the Voidwalker (lean End-assassin plate).** Blue-violet night steel under a void-cloth hood: a tapered
  hood with an amethyst-rimmed opening, a shadowed face with enderman-magenta eyes, a shinobi mask, and a diamond rune
  on the back. A V-collared breastplate with a teal Eye-of-Ender clasp, stepped lames and a segmented spine with
  shoulder blades. Hidden blades on both forearms. A heavy left pauldron growing violet end crystals, with a glowing
  hem. Split coat tails and side panels that move with the legs. Slim greaves with raised shin guards, and pointed
  sabatons.

- **Blood, the Blood Knight** (after Tad's Warhammer reference). Crimson heavy plate with gold filigree over
  blackened iron. A closed helm with a pointed visor and a dim ember eye slit, crimson bat-wing crests with gold
  claw tips, and a crest fin. Three-tier pauldrons with a gold-tipped spike, a gold chevron and blood jewel on the
  breast, and a black winged device on the back. A gold skull at the belt, gold poleyns with a forward spike, and
  pointed sabatons.
- **Evocation, the spell-knight** (after Tad's reference, with emerald accents). A closed dark-steel helm with
  engraved gold panels, a grille visor, a nasal guard and a gold brow set with an emerald, over a black hood. Steel
  pauldrons with engraved gold panels and an emerald, and raised neck guards. A brown leather cuirass with engraved
  chest panels under buckled straps (emerald on the upper buckle), purple sleeves and leather bracers with gold
  plates. A purple sash and belt with an emerald buckle, and a sash tail that swings with the left leg. A
  leather-scale front panel and engraved side tassets, steel knee cops with emeralds, engraved greaves, and leather
  boots with gold toe caps set with emeralds.
- **Nature, the overgrown knight** (after Tad's reference). Old dark plate the forest has claimed: moss on every
  upper surface and creeping up the greaves from the ground. Leaf growth bursts from the shoulders, collar, chest
  corners, back, tassets, knees, forearms and boot cuffs. It uses real cutout foliage, see-through like vanilla
  leaves (GeckoLib armour renders with `armorCutoutNoCull`, so alpha-0 texels are holes). An antler crown rises over
  a leafy wreath, with a dim green life-glow in a stern visor and a red rose at the breast. A twisted-vine belt, and
  a vine with glowing berries winding round the left forearm only. No shield or cloak, per Tad.
- **Eldritch:** placeholder plate in its colours until its own pass (faceless abyssal plate).

## Verified
1. Both `.geo.json` files parse and bake with the real GeckoLib 4.8.4 classes (`GeometryTree` +
   `BakedModelFactory`), and every required bone exists with the right parent (scratch `BakeProbe`).
2. The generator refuses UV overlaps, unpainted texels and fractional box sizes, and nudges cubes so no two armour
   faces are coplanar where they overlap (no z-fighting between armour pieces).
3. In-game screenshots (dev client, Tad's test world): lineup, close front, close back, first person and third
   person for both sets, in `docs/art/ingame_v2_*.png`.
